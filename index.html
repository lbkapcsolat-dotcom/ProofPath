Earth Evidence

An environmental evidence-learning tool that asks a disciplined question: does the evidence support the claim, contradict it, or remain insufficient — and what evidence should come next?

What changed from ProofPath

This NextStep build adds an Earth-specific training and holdout corpus, environmental category detection, and a new Next evidence needed step that turns a verdict into a concrete research question.

Why it matters

Environmental debates often mix partial observations with broad conclusions. Earth Evidence is designed to teach restraint: identify support, contradiction, and missing evidence before making a stronger claim.

Model status

Claim ceiling: EDUCATIONAL_ENVIRONMENTAL_EVIDENCE_ASSESSMENT_ONLY. Model scores are not calibrated scientific confidence. This prototype is not a climate model, scientific validator, policy recommendation engine, or automatic fact-checking service.
