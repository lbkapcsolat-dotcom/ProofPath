Classifier authority

The bundled three-class model decides SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, or INSUFFICIENT. Gemini is never allowed to alter that verdict or its probabilities.

Gemini learning layer

After a verdict exists, Gemini may explain evidence limits in student-friendly language and suggest one next evidence-gathering step.

Model status
