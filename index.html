ProofPath

An educational AI/ML tool that teaches one question: does the evidence support the claim, contradict it, or remain insufficient?

READY
Choose an example or enter your own claim and evidence.

How the ML works

A small three-class softmax regression model is trained in the browser from bundled labeled claim–evidence examples. It learns from overlap, negation, numeric conflict, uncertainty and overclaim signals.

Why it is educational

ProofPath never presents itself as a truth detector. It teaches students to separate support, contradiction, and missing evidence.

Model status

Claim ceiling: EDUCATIONAL_EVIDENCE_ASSESSMENT_ONLY. This prototype is not a scientific validator, truth detector, or automatic fact-checking service.
