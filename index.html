Claim ceiling

What EcoDrift does not claim

EcoDrift is a descriptive signal explorer. It does not attribute climate change, forecast weather or drought, measure real-world drought impact, or prove causality. A CUSUM peak is a candidate structural break, not a certified regime change.

The uncertainty card is deliberately fail-closed: if the bootstrap interval crosses zero, the trend is labeled unresolved.
