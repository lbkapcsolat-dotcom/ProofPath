# ProofPath Mini Model Card

Model: three-class multiclass logistic regression / softmax regression.
Classes: SUPPORTED / CONTRADICTED / INSUFFICIENT.
Training: bundled synthetic educational claim–evidence pairs.
Holdout: 10 fixed examples not present in the training set.
Measured bundled result: 10/10.
Intended use: teaching evidence reasoning.
Out of scope: truth detection, scientific validation, medical/legal/high-stakes decisions.
Limitation: tiny synthetic dataset; no claim of broad real-world generalization.
